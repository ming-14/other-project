### 步骤

1. 下载 mingw-lite 的发行版，如 [mingw64-ucrt-15.2.0-r1.7z](https://github.com/redpanda-cpp/mingw-lite/releases/download/15.2.0-r1/mingw64-ucrt-15.2.0-r1.7z)

2. 下载语言包 [kawaii.mo](https://github.com/Bill-Haku/kawaii-gcc/releases/download/v0.6.0/zh-kawaii.mo)

3. 复制 `kawaii.mo` 至 `mingw64-ucrt\share\locale\zh_CN\LC_MESSAGES\gcc.mo`，`kawaii.mo` 要重命名为 `gcc.mo`
